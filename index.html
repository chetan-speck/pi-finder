Infinite digits. A finite moment.

Pi never ends, but no computer can finish an infinite calculation. It has not been proved that every finite sequence occurs in pi. Longer numbers may take far more time than your device can practically provide.

A few things to know

Pause keeps your session in memory. Resume and Keep exploring use a fresh allowance equal to your selected timeout. Stop, New search, closing or reloading the page discard the calculation state. Background tabs and suspended devices can delay computation and timers.

We keep recent digits, not the whole expansion. The arbitrary-precision arithmetic state still grows, so speed and memory become practical limits. If the worker cannot respond to a timeout or pause within two seconds, it is terminated and its state cannot be resumed. Reported counts then reflect the last confirmed update.

Active calculation time measures worker calculation slices; the timeout allowance measures elapsed wall time while running. Numbers never go into URLs, analytics, logs or storage.
